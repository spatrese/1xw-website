1XW Model

The 1XW Model serves as the framework's portfolio layer, converting research insights into a structured cross-asset strategy. It features a tracked NAV, defined risk boundaries, and a documented history of trade activity.
NAV tracks the cumulative performance of the model portfolio, while PLB represents the system’s performance lower boundary within the risk-control framework. The gap between NAV and PLB provides a simple visual measure of portfolio cushion versus downside threshold.
